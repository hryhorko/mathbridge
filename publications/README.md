# Publications

This section lists research publications related to the development of the
MathBridge EdTech ecosystem and its technological components.

The publications document different stages of the project, from the analysis
and selection of open-source educational technologies to the implementation
and experimental evaluation of the ATutor–JupyterHub integration.

---

## 1. On the deployment of an open-source digital learning ecosystem

**Hryhorii Habrusiev, Vasyl Oleksiuk, Pavlo Chopyk, Iryna Habrusieva, 
Sergii Alilujko**

The article presents the experience of developing, implementing, and testing
an open-source digital learning ecosystem for students of technical
specialties.

The study analyzes potential open-source and commercial components and
justifies the use of open-source technologies for educational institutions.
The proposed ecosystem integrates:

- ATutor LMS for educational content management;
- SageMath for computer algebra and mathematical computation;
- GeoGebra for interactive visualization.

The ecosystem was implemented and piloted under real university conditions.
The study also discusses possible directions for further development,
including learning analytics, mobile access, and expansion of LMS
functionality.

**DOI:**  
https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21275446

**Publication:**  
Habrusiev, H., Oleksiuk, V., Chopyk, P., Habrusieva, I., & Alilujko, S.
*On the deployment of an open-source digital learning ecosystem.*

**Role in the MathBridge research:**  
This publication represents an earlier stage of the research project. It
establishes the motivation for using open-source technologies in a digital
learning ecosystem and describes the initial integration of ATutor, SageMath,
and GeoGebra.

The current MathBridge research extends this work by focusing on the
integration of ATutor with JupyterHub and on a JWT-based Single Sign-On
mechanism for seamless access to isolated computational environments.

## 2. ATutor–JupyterHub Integration: A JWT-Based SSO Approach

**Current research publication**

This work describes the development and experimental evaluation of the
ATutor–JupyterHub integration implemented as part of the MathBridge EdTech
project.

The study focuses on:

- JWT-based Single Sign-On;
- integration of ATutor with JupyterHub;
- custom JupyterHub authentication;
- Docker-based isolation of user environments;
- cold-start and warm-start access behavior;
- statistical evaluation of access-time differences;
- limitations of the current single-node architecture.

The work represents the next stage of the MathBridge research following the
initial open-source ecosystem described above.
