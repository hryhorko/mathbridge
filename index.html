About the Project

MathBridge is an innovative, open-source EdTech solution designed to transform mathematics education for engineering students. The platform seamlessly integrates the ATutor Learning Management System (LMS) with a multi-user JupyterHub environment, provisioning isolated Docker containers for every user upon secure, single-sign-on web authorization.

Pedagogical Innovation

Instead of utilizing advanced mathematical tools like SageMath and GeoGebra as basic "calculators", MathBridge uses interactive Jupyter Notebooks as conceptual bridges. Students link abstract mathematical objects (matrices, differential equations, integrals) directly with simulated engineering processes and code-driven laboratory tasks.

Current Stage & Pilot Deployment

Status: Prototype Validated (MVP)

The initial architecture has been successfully tested under a local environment with up to 10 active concurrent users. We are currently scaling the infrastructure to launch a 1-year educational pilot experiment for 50+ active engineering students at Ternopil Ivan Puluj National Technical University (TNTU), Ukraine, starting in September 2026.
